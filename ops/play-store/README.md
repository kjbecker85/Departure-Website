# Google Play store graphics (Glacier look)

Made 2026-11-27 from the Departure app on main (version 1.1.4), in the Glacier look the app moved to on 2026-11-26. The screens come from the browser build running against a local Supabase stack with made-up demo players (Nova and the team Iron Wolves). No production data was used. The browser build is close to the phone app but not identical.

Phone screenshots are PNG, 1080 by 1920 (9:16), each under 1 MB. Upload them in this order.

| File | Label | Caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01-home.png` | Home | Open the app to see your rank, your coins and your next step. |
| `02-log-workout.png` | Log a workout | Log every set in seconds and see the XP you earn before you finish. |
| `03-train.png` | Train | See every day you trained and keep your weekly streak going. |
| `04-arena.png` | Arena | Choose your fight: train solo, duel other players or take on monsters. |
| `05-team-warpath.png` | Team Warpath | Every workout you log helps your team beat 24 bosses. |
| `06-equip.png` | Equip | Workouts and battles drop armor and weapons for your warrior. |
| `07-shop.png` | Armory Shop | Spend the coins you earn from training on new gear in the shop. |
| `08-ranks.png` | Ranks | Climb 24 ranks from Recruit to Legend as your XP grows. |

Feature graphic: `feature-graphic.png`, PNG, 1024 by 500, no transparency. It shows the name Departure, the label Fitness Quest, the line "Every workout you log makes your warrior stronger." and two rank warriors from the app's own art. It does not use the app icon, which is still purple and does not match Glacier.

Not covered: Future Self (it needs a real selfie and the image function) and a boss fight result screen.
